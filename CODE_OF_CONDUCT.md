# Code of Conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

Treat contributors and users respectfully, regardless of background or experience. Keep criticism specific and focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and unauthorized disclosure of private information are not acceptable. Do not share account credentials or private relationship data, or use the project to harass or manipulate others.

Maintainers may warn, remove content or restrict participation according to severity. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact channel on their [public profile](https://github.com/iruochen); do not disclose an affected person's private information in public discussion.
